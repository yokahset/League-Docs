## Rule Explanations:

1. Superflex:
> Adoption of this format would add, or convert a flex spot, into a "superflex" spot to the roster where any position, including quarterback, can be played. 
2. Removing 3rd Round Reversal:
> Currently, every 3rd round rookie pick has been in reverse order where the owner of the worst team that picks 1.01, picks 3.10 and the best team that picks 1.10 picks 3.01. While we originally had good reasons for this, it has become extremely cumbersome to execute as it requires manually tracking positions in a way sleeper doesn't help with, and a lot of triple checking to ensure traded picks are in the proper positions, etc. 
>
> Elimination of this rule will give bad teams more high picks, good teams more low picks, and both commissioners a much easier time administrating the league. 
3. Extending the Trade Deadline:
> At the onset of this league, the NFL was a 16 week league. 
> When it added the 17th week, it moved its own trade deadline back one week into the season from week 8 to week 9. 
> 
>Moving the fantasy trade deadline back one week from week 10 to week 11 means that teams will once again get TWO weeks to evaluate after a player has been traded instead of the ONE week it has been reduced to. 
>
> Our league has not extended the season by a game in response to the 17 game season, so this also means that the buying and selling deadline is much closer to the playoffs than it would have ever been before, leaving teams to stock up later and sell off later than usual.
4. Changing the Rookie Draft to a Slow Draft: 
> Sad Fact: We have NEVER in this league had a SINGLE draft without a pick being auto drafted. Speaking for both of your commissioners, we think this hurts out league, hurts your fair and honest chances at a fair and fun draft experience, and deprives us of a chance to play with out friends decisions, and not a robot's. 
>
> Life is busy, a three, five, or ten minute timer doesn't always let us participate fully in our drafts. A slow draft would help each of us be able to make each pick with intentionality, and also allow for draft-day trades, something we've never previously been able to do without extreme stress from the limited clock.
